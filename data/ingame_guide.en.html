Using the in-game “build order”

This matches “Next item / After that” on the card; here is how to read it and buy in order in game.

1. Open the shop

Press F3 in game (or click the shop icon at the bottom-right). The shop has Guide and Recommended tabs at the top.

2. Read the recommended build

  1. Open the Guide tab: popular builds for the current patch are listed on the left.
  2. Some builds show 1 → 2 → 3: buy in that order and finish the first item first.
  3. Without numbers, the Recommended grid reads left to right by priority.
The card recommends one item at a time (with reason, usage and skill build). After you buy it, the next refresh moves to the next item — the same idea as a long in-game build, split into single steps.

3. Where our order comes from

  • Build sequences extracted from Bilibili guides come first (Settings → Assist shows the count and update time).
  • Without guide data we fall back to OpenDota’s popular items for the current patch.
  • We filter by your hero’s kit: heroes with a built-in dispel are not told to buy Manta, and if you already own BKB we stop pushing more control items.

4. Tips

  • Ctrl + left-click an item to quick-buy and deliver it to your hero.
  • In the starting-items phase buy tangos/salve from Consumables first, then your first small item.
  • If the threat changes, use the reason on the card to swap in a counter item (e.g. lots of silence → BKB/Manta).

This page may change between patches; if it differs from the client, trust the in-game Guide/Recommended tabs.
